How it works

Everything passes, except what you protect

Spikes402 answers protected paths with HTTP 402 Payment Required and two ways in. Everything else goes through untouched.

A bot asks for a page

On a protected path it gets a 402 with a Lightning invoice (L402) and a proof-of-work puzzle in the WWW-Authenticate headers.

It pays or it works

A few sats to your own node, or a sha256d puzzle sized to the price: seconds of CPU instead of free bulk crawling.

The page opens

The proof is checked locally in microseconds and spent once. People get the same choice on a friendly page.

For people

Pay or PoW, in the browser

Browsers get a page instead of JSON: the invoice first (QR code, Open in wallet, one click with WebLN), and No sats? Do the challenge instead.

  • One moment. The browser solves the puzzle in Web Workers and shows the work: hashes per second, the best hash, a grid that fills up.
  • Paid with a phone? The page notices the payment by itself and opens.
  • Three modes: button (default), auto or off.
  • Nothing from outside: strict Content-Security-Policy, hashed inline scripts only.
New in this fork

Every proof of work is a lottery ticket

With lottery set, Spikes402 joins a solo mining pool (stratum v1) with your Bitcoin address. Puzzles are real block headers: the same double SHA-256 and the same cost, but a hash good enough for the network would be a whole block, paid to you.

// config.json
"lottery": {
  "pool": "solo.ckpool.org:3333",
  "address": "bc1q…",
  "worker": "mysite"
},
"browser_pow": { "mode": "button" }
  • Bots get a second challenge, algorithm="bitcoin"; old clients keep solving the ordinary one.
  • Browsers play too: the 402 page computes on the header and says so.
  • Shares that clear the pool's target are submitted; the dashboard counts them.

Under the hood

Small, defensive, yours

Lightning L402

Invoices from your own phoenixd node with a receive-only password. Spikes402 never holds anyone's money.

sha256d or Argon2id

Proof-of-work priced in seconds per sat; memory-hard Argon2id when you want GPUs to sweat.

Bot identification

Verified crawlers by IP lists, reverse DNS and Web Bot Auth signatures; impostors are caught.

Node protection

Circuit breaker, global rate limits and caps on open invoices: a flood cannot drown your node.

Surge pricing

Optional: prices rise with sustained demand against each group's own baseline.

Browser wallet

A prototype extension pays trusted sites in one click, within daily caps you set.

Get started

Try it in a minute

With the fake Lightning backend: no node, no real money. A stand-in website on port 8080, the proxy on 8402, and the example bot.

Experimental prototype. Not independently reviewed: do not use it with real money on mainnet yet.
